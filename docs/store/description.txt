Select text. Middle-click. Search.

Middle Click to Search opens selected text in a new tab using your preferred search engine. Select a web address to open it directly.

Make it work your way:
• Choose Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Brave, or a custom search URL.
• Open searches in the foreground or background.
• Choose foreground or background behavior for ordinary middle-clicked links.
• Hold Ctrl or Command to reverse tab focus.
• Enable or disable the extension on individual sites, or use an allow/block list.
• Optionally copy selected text to the clipboard when searching.
• Keep your selection, prevent autoscroll, or always search text that resembles a URL.

The toolbar popup provides quick controls and page status. A red ! badge indicates a failed action; open the popup for details. The settings page highlights unsaved changes.

Preferences sync through Chrome. There are no ads, analytics, accounts, or extension-operated servers. Search text goes to your chosen search engine when you open a search.

Refresh existing webpages after installing or updating. Browser settings pages, the Chrome Web Store, and other protected pages do not support the gesture. Requires Chrome 116 or newer. A middle-click means pressing the mouse wheel.

Source and support: https://github.com/DovieW/middle-click-search
